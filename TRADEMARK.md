# Gotoquill name and logo policy

**Approved by Shay Levi on 23 November 2026 (Asia/Jerusalem). Not legal advice. Prepared in the repository, not published on the live site.**

The Gotoquill name and logo identify Shay Levi's project and official service. The trademark is not registered. Registration with the Israel Patent Office (ILPO), or elsewhere, is deferred for Shay to decide later. This policy does not claim registered trademark status.

Apache 2.0 is the license for the planned Local code release. It does not grant permission to use the Gotoquill name or logo as your own product or service identity. Under this policy, forks and unofficial hosts must choose a different name, must not call themselves Gotoquill, and must not imply official status or endorsement. Do not use the logo to make an unofficial service appear official.

Truthful attribution is allowed, such as "Based on Gotoquill, modified by Dana Cohen." Retain required license and NOTICE attribution. This is consistent with the reasonable and customary attribution allowed by section 6 of Apache 2.0; it does not restrict the code license or any rights supplied by applicable law.
